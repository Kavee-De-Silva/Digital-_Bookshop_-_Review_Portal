About Us

A brief look at the history of DigiBook and our purpose.


Our Story

DigiBook is a modern digital bookstore designed for Sri Lankan readers who love to read. Our primary goal is to create an environment where you can easily and securely purchase and read any e-book or audiobook you want, without having to physically visit a bookstore.



🎯 Our Vision

Providing every person in Sri Lanka with the opportunity to access the world's best books from anywhere through technology.

🚀 Our Mission

Bringing books by local and foreign authors closer to the reader through digital technology at a very reasonable price and with high quality.
